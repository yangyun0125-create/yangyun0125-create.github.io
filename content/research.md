## Technological Innovation and Agricultural Sustainability

My doctoral research examines the role of technological innovation in agricultural sustainability transitions. I am interested in how innovation can support agricultural production while responding to environmental challenges.

## Green Productivity and Environmental Performance

I am interested in the relationship between productivity and environmental performance in agriculture. This research direction considers how assessments of economic performance can account for environmental pressures and resource use.

## Productivity and Efficiency Measurement

I am interested in methods for measuring productivity and efficiency, including Data Envelopment Analysis (DEA). These approaches provide a framework for examining differences in performance and identifying opportunities for improvement.

## Comparative Agricultural Research: China and the European Union

My research has a particular focus on comparative evidence from China and the European Union. I am interested in how different agricultural and economic contexts shape the relationship between innovation, productivity, and sustainability.

## Methods and Tools

- Data Envelopment Analysis (DEA)
- Productivity Measurement
- Econometric Analysis
- Panel Data Methods

These are methodological interests for my ongoing and planned research. The directions above describe research questions and plans; they do not present completed findings.
