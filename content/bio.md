I am a PhD student at **Institut Agro Dijon** and **INRAE CESAER (UMR 1041)** in Dijon, France. My research examines the role of technological innovation in agricultural sustainability transitions, with a focus on productivity and environmental performance.

I am particularly interested in comparative evidence from **China and the European Union**, and in how economic analysis can inform a more sustainable agricultural sector.

Before starting my PhD, I completed a master's degree in Industrial Economics at Anhui University of Finance and Economics and a bachelor's degree in Business Administration at Southwest Petroleum University in China.
